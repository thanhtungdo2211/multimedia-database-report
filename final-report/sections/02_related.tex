\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Text-based person search.} Li~\textit{et al.}~\cite{li2017personsearchnaturallanguage} introduced the task with CUHK-PEDES; ICFG-PEDES~\cite{ding2021semanticallyselfalignednetworktexttoimage} and RSTPReid~\cite{Zhu_2021}, both built from MSMT17 imagery, followed. Early methods paired separately pre-trained image and text networks and aligned them with identity-aware objectives, most notably the cross-modal projection matching and classification losses CMPM/CMPC~\cite{Zhang_2018}, often with explicit part- or phrase-level alignment~\cite{ding2021semanticallyselfalignednetworktexttoimage}. CLIP changed the starting point. CFine~\cite{yan2022clipdrivenfinegrainedtextimageperson} and the empirical study of Cao~\textit{et al.}~\cite{cao2023empiricalstudycliptextbased} show that a jointly pre-trained image--text model outperforms these specialised designs, and IRRA~\cite{jiang2023crossmodalimplicitrelationreasoning} combines full CLIP fine-tuning with masked-language-model relation reasoning and the SDM loss. We keep IRRA's backbone and loss and drop everything else.

\textbf{Parameter-efficient transfer.} PETL freezes a pre-trained model and trains a small inserted component: bottleneck adapters~\cite{houlsby2019parameterefficienttransferlearningnlp}, learned prefixes~\cite{li2021prefixtuningoptimizingcontinuousprompts}, or LoRA~\cite{hu2021loralowrankadaptationlarge}, which adds a trainable low-rank update to frozen weight matrices that can be merged into them after training. For TBPS, CSKT~\cite{liu2024clipbasedsynergisticknowledgetransfer} transfers CLIP knowledge with prompts and adapters, DM-Adapter~\cite{liu2025dmadapterdomainawaremixtureofadapterstextbased} routes features through a mixture of adapters, and UP-Person~\cite{liu2025uppersonunifiedparameterefficienttransfer} unifies LoRA, prefixes and adapters. Each trains several million parameters across several modules and losses. We use one LoRA configuration an order of magnitude smaller and one loss, so the role of the loss can be measured.

\textbf{Federated TBPS.} FedAvg~\cite{mcmahan2023communicationefficientlearningdeepnetworks} trains a shared model by averaging client updates, so the size of the trainable part sets the communication cost of each round. FedSH~\cite{Ma_2024}, the first federated TBPS framework, uses ResNet-50 and Bi-LSTM backbones and exchanges only part of the model to cut that cost; it also notes that two-tower models double the transmission per round. We are not aware of federated TBPS work that pairs a CLIP backbone with PEFT. \Cref{sec:fl} estimates what LoRA would save in that setting.
